# Storyboard

Visual system: light gray world, charcoal type, green (#1E9E58) and orange (#F0832A) accents, glass panels with soft shadows, Space Grotesk titles, Inter UI text, JetBrains Mono for data. The AI Agent is a glossy green "core" with two soft eyes and orbit rings, never a robot. Scenes are joined by match cuts and object transformations, not fades.

## 1. Hook (0.0–4.45): `compositions/s1-hook.html`
Live video of a busy Cambodian employee (Veo, from a Gemini image). Glass windows build up around her: inbox with a climbing unread count, browser tabs, spreadsheet, calendar, chat bubbles, stacked notifications, a clock racing from 09:00 to 17:45, and URGENT / +12 TASKS / OVERDUE tags. The camera pushes toward the laptop, then zooms through the lid. The windows fly past the camera into a light flash.

## 2. AI Agent (4.45–11.55): `compositions/s2-agent.html`
We land inside the screen. The prompt bar shimmers, then "Prepare today's business report." types in sync with the narration. On send, the letters arc away and break into data particles that converge into the AI Agent core (burst ring, rings spin up, eyes open). The kinetic title AI AGENT appears with a green-to-orange underline, a status chip types "Working on: today's business report", and tool icons orbit the agent. The agent shrinks to its post for scene 3.

## 3. Understand → Plan → Execute (11.55–19.09): `compositions/s3-understand.html`
The camera tracks along a pipeline, with parallax giant words and foreground depth dots.
- **Instruction:** the prompt card.
- **Understand:** a scan beam reads the sentence, highlights "today's" and "business report", and extracts task chips.
- **Plan:** the chips fly over and become a numbered 5-step plan.
- **Execute:** steps tick off with spinners turning into checks, and a progress bar climbs.
- **Exit:** the camera pulls back to the whole pipeline, then the Execute node grows into the full-screen workspace.

## 4. Use tools (19.09–29.76): `compositions/s4-tools.html`
- **Split screen:** the chatbot "only answers with text" (greyed out), while the AI Agent and the kinetic "USE TOOLS" appear and six tools burst out of the agent.
- **Desktop:** the tools morph into a dock and the agent condenses into a green "AI Agent" cursor. It opens a browser and types a search, scans a PDF, fills a spreadsheet, and books "4:00 PM · Report review" in the calendar. Each result flies into a "Collected" tray.
- **Pull back:** the screen tilts into 3D and the windows tile like a real workstation. The collected items fly out toward scene 5.

## 5. Take action (29.76–39.54): `compositions/s5-action.html`
- **Title:** "TAKE ACTION" lands, then the collected chips stream into a spreadsheet that fills row by row (total $48,920).
- **Chart:** the Sales cells lift out and morph into bars, and a growth line draws itself.
- **Report:** the chart drops into a self-assembling Daily Business Report (header, KPI count-ups, summary, next steps, stacked pages).
- **Close:** "NOT JUST TALK." is struck through, "REAL WORK." lands, then a COMPLETED stamp and the Business_Report.pdf chip appear. Everything is carried upward into scene 6.

## 6. AI team (39.54–48.82): `compositions/s6-team.html`
A tilted 3D table holds the "Monthly business review" assignment. It splits into four color-coded tasks that fly to the Research, Finance, HR and Reporting agents. Lines draw out from the lead agent and packets travel along them. All four agents work in parallel (search rows, revenue count-up and bars, staff check-ins, document writing), with progress rings completing at different times. Results flow back along the lines and the lead agent emits a "Final result: Daily Business Report" card, which glides left into scene 7.

## 7. Human review (48.82–55.30): `compositions/s7-review.html`
Live video of a Cambodian manager reviewing on his tablet (Veo). The result card grows into the HUMAN REVIEW panel. As he reviews, the checklist ticks: Data sources verified, Numbers checked, Recommendations reviewed. The "Manager" cursor clicks Approve on the word "approve", the button turns green, the APPROVED stamp lands, and a soft glow follows. Footer: "Final decision: Manager".

## 8. Final message (55.30–61.50): `compositions/s8-final.html`
The film opens on the exact last frame of scene 7. The camera pulls back fast through a 3D mosaic of the whole AI workspace (recap frames from every scene), with data streams in the gaps. The kinetic lines land on the narration: HUMAN LEADS. / AI AGENTS EXECUTE. / WORK GETS DONE. An orange underline draws, then the "AI For Business" sign-off appears and everything closes to a clean light frame.

## Audio
`assets/audio/soundtrack.wav`: a single master at -14 LUFS integrated, -1.2 dBFS peak.
- **Voice:** Khmer narration with TTS edge artifacts trimmed, matched to -16 LUFS, light EQ.
- **Music:** ducked smoothly under the voice (8–16 dB below it).
- **Effects:** about 150 synthesized cues placed on the animation times (typing, clicks, notification pings, whooshes, data sparkles, impacts, success chimes). Effects step back 3 dB while the narrator speaks.
